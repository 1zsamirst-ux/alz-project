THE CHALLENGE

Understanding Alzheimer's disease requires more than one signal.

Alzheimer's disease is complex. Biological changes, cognitive performance and clinical characteristics may provide different pieces of information about an individual's cognitive health.

ALZ Project explores how these different types of information can be studied together using responsible data analysis.

RESEARCH OUTPUT

Investigating a Cognitive Risk Score

A proposed analytical framework to explore patterns in cognitive risk. The approach requires testing and validation before any clinical interpretation.

CRS is a research concept under development, not a clinically validated score or diagnostic tool.

ABOUT ALZ PROJECT

Turning complex data
into research insights.

ALZ Project is an emerging research initiative exploring how biological markers, cognitive measures and data science can contribute to a better understanding of Alzheimer's disease.

Our focus is on research, responsible data analysis and the exploration of new approaches.
